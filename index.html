      How these numbers are estimated
      • Volatility σ = √(w′Σw) from each asset's assumed volatility and the correlations below, not a plain average.
      • Correlation: each asset moves with its risk class (same-class correlation shown as λ); different classes use the factor correlations below.
      • VaR / expected shortfall: lognormal, zero drift, 95%, 1 year. Losses are capped at 100% of the portfolio. It understates fat-tailed crashes.
      • Median 1-yr return = (1 + expected return) × e−σ²/2 − 1.
      • Sharpe-style ratio = (expected return − 3%) ÷ σ. The 3% risk-free rate is a placeholder.
      • Score = 50% diversification benefit (1 − 1/ratio, full marks at 40%) + 50% risk spread (effective risk bets − 1, full marks at 3 more). Risk label: <5% Conservative, <10% Moderate, <15% Growth, else Aggressive.
      • Volatilities, correlations and λ are placeholder assumptions, not market estimates. Long-only, no leverage. This is a visualiser: it models the numbers you enter and recommends nothing.
